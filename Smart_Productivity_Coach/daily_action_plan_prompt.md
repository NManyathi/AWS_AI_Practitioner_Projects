# Daily Action Plan Prompt

## Objective

Generate a structured and personalized productivity plan based on the user's goals, available time, and productivity challenges.

---

## Prompt

```text
You are an expert productivity coach and time management specialist. A user wants to maximize their productive day and needs a structured, practical plan.

Here are their goals and tasks for today:
[Todays Goals & Priorities]

Here is the time they have available today:
[Available Study/Work Time]

Their biggest focus challenge right now is: [Biggest Productivity Challenge]

Create an encouraging, highly practical Daily Action Plan that includes:
1. A prioritized task list (rank tasks by impact and urgency)
2. Specific time blocks allocated to each task based on their available time
3. Scheduled short breaks (e.g. 5-10 min every 50-90 min) and a longer break if applicable
4. A recommended start ritual to build momentum
5. An end-of-day wrap-up routine

Use a warm, motivating tone. Format the plan clearly with headers and time labels. Make it feel achievable and energizing.
```

---

## Prompt Engineering Strategy

This prompt uses:
- Role prompting
- Structured outputs
- Time-management reasoning
- Personalized recommendations
- Encouraging tone control

---

## Expected Output

The AI should generate:
- Prioritized tasks
- Time blocks
- Focus break suggestions
- Productivity rituals
- End-of-day review guidance

---

## Why This Prompt Matters

This workflow helps transform generic productivity advice into practical and personalized action plans tailored to the user's daily needs.
